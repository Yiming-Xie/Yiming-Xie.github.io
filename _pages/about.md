I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Automation at <a href="https://www.tsinghua.edu.cn/en/">Tsinghua University</a>, advised by Prof. <a href="https://www.au.tsinghua.edu.cn/info/1111/1524.htm">Xiangyang Ji</a>.

My research focuses on <b>computational imaging</b> and <b>computer vision</b>. I am interested in co-designing <b>optics and AI</b> for task-driven imaging and perception. My work spans physics-based imaging simulation, synthetic data generation, and end-to-end differentiable optical design.

Previously, I received my Master's degree from Tsinghua University, also advised by Prof. Ji, and my B.E. from <a href="https://www.seu.edu.cn/english/">Southeast University</a>, where I was advised by Prof. <a href="https://www.yangangwang.com/">Yangang Wang</a>.

I am currently collaborating with Prof. <a href="https://scholar.google.fi/citations?user=3T9v3NYAAAAJ&amp;hl=zh-CN&amp;oi=sra">Qilin Sun</a> and Xianhong Zhao.
